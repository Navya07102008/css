    • Kuchipudi traces its roots to the ancient Hindu Sanskrit text, the Natya Shastra. Historically developed as a religious art form by wandering bards and temple artists, it matured into a distinct dance-drama tradition in the 17 century under the guidance of Siddhendra Yogi. While early performances required male troupes, it has since evolved into a celebrated style performed by both men and women, requiring rigorous training in acting, music, and rhythmic footwork. Kuchipudi is the preeminent classical dance form of Andhra Pradesh. Characterized by graceful eye movements, intricate footwork, and dramatic storytelling, it blends pure dance, expressive mime, and theatrical dialogue. Traditionally rooted in Hindu mythology and the Sanskrit text Natya Shastra, it remains a vibrant cornerstone of South Indian cultural heritage.
